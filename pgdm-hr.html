Program Overview

Shaping HR leaders for a people-first world

Organizations today are increasingly focused on holistic workforce management, employee engagement, and the adoption of HR technology. The role of HR professionals has gained greater significance across industries.

"Train people well enough so they can leave. Treat them well enough so they don't want to." — Sir Richard Branson

The PGDM at ABS equips future HR leaders with advanced knowledge, practical tools, and cross-cultural sensitivity — covering Organizational Behaviour, HR Metrics & Analytics, Rewards & Performance Management, Labour Laws, International HRM and Change Management, delivered through case studies, simulations, live projects and industry guest sessions. Curriculum

6 Key Specializations

Discover the core academic pathways tailored to build global strategic HR leaders.

International Human Resource Management

▼
Equips future HR professionals to manage diverse global teams, navigate cross-cultural workplace complexities, and implement strategic international recruitment, compliance, and compensation policies across multinational organizations.

Industrial Relations & Labour Laws

▼
Offers comprehensive insights into employee relations, trade unions, dispute resolution, and legal compliance frameworks. Focuses on bridging industrial gaps and maintaining a harmonious, legally compliant working environment.

Human Resource Development

▼
Focuses on strategic talent development, employee training programs, performance coaching, and learning methodologies. Prepares professionals to unlock workforce potential and drive continuous organizational learning and growth.

Organizational Change & Development

▼
Prepares leaders to guide businesses through structural transformations, mergers, and cultural shifts. Teaches OD diagnostics, intervention techniques, and strategies to build resilient, agile corporate cultures.

HR Metrics & Analytics

▼
Empowers managers to utilize workforce data for predictive analytics, performance mapping, and strategic business decisions. Focuses on data-driven HR metrics that optimize talent acquisition and employee retention.

Performance Management & Compensation

▼
Covers strategic design of appraisal systems, performance rewards, employee benefits, and competitive compensation architectures. Focuses on aligning individual performance outcomes with overarching business growth strategies.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is PGDM in Human Resource Management? +
A specialized 2-year postgraduate program designed to build core competencies in strategic human capital development, staffing, performance appraisal systems, HR Analytics, employee relations, and labour policies.
What is the duration and structure of the program? +
It is a full-time, 2-year residential program divided into four semesters. The pedagogy comprises classroom instruction, simulation exercises, live internships, and an international learning trip.
What details are available for installment payments & loans? +
The base academic fee is ₹9.5 Lakhs. We offer flexible term payments and zero-cost EMI financing plans in partnership with top student loan agencies starting at ₹18,500/month.
Is this PGDM program valid and accredited? +
Yes. The program is fully approved by the AICTE, accredited by the NBA, and certified by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) as equivalent to an MBA degree.
