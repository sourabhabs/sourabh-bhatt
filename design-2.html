Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the PGDM degree equivalence, placements, and Oxford tour.

Yes. Asian Business School is AICTE-approved, NBA-accredited, and the PGDM program holds equivalence to an MBA granted by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). It is also recognized internationally by corporate recruiters and AACSB member institutions.
The study trip is a 1-week fully paid residential educational program to Oxford/London, UK. Students attend academic sessions at Oxford/London and earn an Executive Diploma in International Business. Flights, accommodation, and curated industry visits are fully covered as part of the curriculum fee structure.
ABS accepts valid percentiles from CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, ATMA, and GMAT. Candidates without an entrance exam scorecard can also appear for the Asian Business School Admission Test (ABSAT) followed by a comprehensive Personal Interview.
For the graduating batch, the highest domestic/international package offered stood at ₹36.64 LPA, with an average CTC of ₹8.03 LPA. More than 550+ prominent companies recruited students across consulting, BFSI, FMCG, and tech verticals.
