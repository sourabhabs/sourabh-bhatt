Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ABS PGDM degree equivalent to an MBA in India and abroad? expand_more
Yes. The PGDM program offered by Asian Business School is AICTE approved, NBA accredited, and recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) as equivalent to an MBA degree, qualifying graduates for both Indian and multinational executive roles and Ph.D. registrations.
Is the Oxford/London UK study trip really 100% sponsored? expand_more
Yes, the 1-week residential learning trip to Oxford/London, UK, is fully covered under the program architecture. It includes academic sessions, certification, factory visits, boarding, and travel arrangements.
What entrance exams are accepted for Batch 2026-28? expand_more
ABS accepts valid scores from CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, ATMA, and GMAT. Candidates who have not appeared for national tests can apply for the ABS Aptitude Assessment Test as part of their evaluation process.
What are the criteria for Merit Scholarships? expand_more
Merit scholarships basis according to composite scores across 10th, 12th, graduation percentages, and percentile in national management entrance exams. Special fee waivers exist for defense wards and academic achievers.
