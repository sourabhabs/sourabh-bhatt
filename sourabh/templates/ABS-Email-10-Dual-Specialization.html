THE CURRICULUM

Two majors. One degree. Double the doors.

Year one has 18 compulsory subjects. You then choose 12 electives, 6 in each of two specializations. Two open electives and 3 compulsory subjects complete the picture.

01 Foundation

18 compulsory subjects in year one.

02 Dual depth

12 electives from Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics.

03 Edge

Open electives in E-Retailing and Micro & Small Business Management.

Your degree is equated to an MBA by AIU. Your combination makes it yours.
